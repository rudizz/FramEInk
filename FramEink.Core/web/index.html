Your photos, on your frame

Choose images from your phone. Previewing, cropping and HEIC → JPEG conversion happen on your phone, even without Internet access.

    Photos on your frame

    View photos stored on the SD card. Select the ones you want to delete.

    Previews load automatically. Tap “View photo” to open a larger image. Deleting removes the selected photos from the SD card permanently, not from your phone.

    Wi‑Fi, weather and calendar settings
    Tap “Finish” when you are done: FramEInk will display the calendar and go to sleep. The portal closes after 10 minutes of inactivity; it stays open during conversion and transfer. To reopen it, turn the frame off and on again. Your photos stay on the SD card.
