FramEInk

Connecting to your device…

Your photos, on your frame

Choose images from your phone. Previewing, cropping and HEIC → JPEG conversion happen on your phone, even without Internet access.

You can select several photos and upload them one at a time.

Zoom is relative to Fit. Use the position sliders to move the image within the frame. Only the preview area is saved.

    Photos on your frame

    View photos stored on the SD card. Select the ones you want to delete.

    Scroll inside the gallery to see more photos. Only visible previews are downloaded; “Select all” also selects photos not yet listed. Tap “View photo” for a larger image. Deleting permanently removes photos from the SD card, not from your phone.

    Photo preview
    Wi‑Fi, weather, calendar, photos and update schedule settings
    Photo playback

    Choose how photos are shown on the frame. Alphabetical follows file names, ignoring case, and starts again after the last photo. Random chooses a photo at each photo update; repeats are possible.

    Hourly updates

    Local time at the frame's weather location. With 00 selected, scheduled updates run at 10:00, 11:00, 12:00, and so on. The initial calendar is still shown after setup. The night pause takes priority.

    Night pause

    Local time at the frame's weather location. No scheduled updates during the pause, including across midnight. Afterwards, updates resume at the selected minute past the hour. Set both times equal to disable the pause; otherwise leave at least one hour outside it.

    Tap “Finish” when you are done: FramEInk will display the calendar and go to sleep. The portal closes after 10 minutes of inactivity; it stays open during conversion and transfer. To reopen it, turn the frame off and on again. Your photos stay on the SD card.
